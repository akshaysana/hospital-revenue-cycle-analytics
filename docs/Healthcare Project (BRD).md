##### Healthcare Project (BRD):



Project Name: Revenue Leakage \& Denial Remediation Analytics

Executive Sponsor: Chief Financial Officer (CFO)

Business Objective: Identify the primary drivers of uncollected revenue by isolating claim denial trends across medical departments and insurance payers, enabling targeted operational interventions.



In-Scope:



* Billed vs. Paid amounts (Revenue Realization).
* Claim statuses (Paid, Denied, Pending).
* Denial Reason Codes.
* Aggregation by Medical Department and Payer.



Out-of-Scope (Phase 2+):



* Time-series AR Aging (30/60/90 day tracking).
* Individual Physician-level profitability.
* Predictive modeling on claim success.
